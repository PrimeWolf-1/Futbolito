# Futbolito

**Futbolito** is a digital adaptation of the traditional Latin American nail soccer board game.

The project is focused on recreating the strategy, competition, and fast-paced gameplay of the physical tabletop version while expanding it into a modern digital experience.

## Vision

Futbolito is designed to become more than a simple game remake.

The long-term vision includes:

- Competitive local and online multiplayer
- Physics-based gameplay mechanics
- Stylized football environments
- Match progression systems
- Tournament and ranking systems
- Social and community-driven gameplay
- Mobile and PC accessibility

The goal is to preserve the nostalgic feel of the original game while modernizing the experience for a wider audience.

## Project Direction
